How It Works

1. Permanent Record

Candidate maintains trade, apprenticeship term, training, projects, systems worked on, credentials and certifications.

2. 30-Day Availability

Available profiles show exact days available and automatically expire after 30 days unless renewed.

3. Anonymous Search

Members see qualifications but no identity or direct contact information.

4. Paid Contact Release

Members purchase contact access based on candidate level.

5. 72-Hour Access

Contact information is available for three days and then locks again.

6. Records Remain

Apprenticeship term, training, project experience and verified credentials remain through the candidate's career.
